Our Story

GreenLeaf Botanics was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: make sustainable, ethically sourced indoor plants accessible to everyone. We partner directly with local growers who share our commitment to organic practices and fair wages.

Every plant ships in 100% compostable packaging, and for each order placed, we plant a tree through our reforestation partners. Together, we've planted over 50,000 trees and counting.
